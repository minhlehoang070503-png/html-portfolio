The best movies according to Cootsup

My three favorite movies recently

Colony (2026)

Here is a science fiction movie. It's about a scientist who despises individual human behavior and wants people to act collectively, like ants. When I first watched it, I was quite surprised by the quality of South Korean cinema, and I saw how the madness of scientists can change the world.

Phi Phong: The Blood Demon (2026)

A Vietnamese horror film about a species of demon believed to still exist in the mountains of the Northwest. The film centers on a family of shamans who specialize in exorcising demons and banishing evil spirits. But in the end, nothing is more evil than the human heart.

Red Rain (2025)

A Vietnamese war film set during the war against the U.S. to liberate the country, taking place in the ancient citadel of Quảng Trị in 1973. The film depicts the brutality of the battlefield, as well as the selflessness and bravery of the soldiers.
