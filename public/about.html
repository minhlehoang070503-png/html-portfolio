About Me

Hello! I'm a first-year Industrial Design and Graphics student with a strong passion for combining creativity, technology, and problem-solving. I enjoy learning new things and constantly challenging myself with projects that require both analytical thinking and creative design. My academic background has given me a solid foundation in mathematics and science, while my interest in design has helped me develop an eye for aesthetics, visual communication, and user experience.

Beyond my coursework, I actively explore programming, especially Python, to build practical applications and automate everyday tasks. I enjoy working with APIs, analyzing data, and creating desktop applications with graphical user interfaces. Through personal projects, I have gained experience working with JSON data, REST APIs, data visualization, and software debugging. I also have a strong interest in graphic design and digital content creation. I regularly use tools such as Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign to create layouts, branding concepts, and visual designs. I believe that good design is not only visually appealing but also functional, intuitive, and centered around the user's needs.

I am particularly fascinated by the intersection of design and technology. Fields such as UI/UX design, technical art, game development, and interactive digital products inspire me because they combine artistic creativity with technical implementation. As someone who enjoys self-directed learning, I frequently study through online courses, technical documentation, and real-world projects. I believe that the best way to master a skill is to build something meaningful while continuously improving through practice and feedback.

Outside of academics, I am working to improve my English communication skills and become more confident in public speaking and teamwork. I see every new project as an opportunity to expand my knowledge, strengthen my problem-solving abilities, and grow both professionally and personally. My long-term goal is to become a multidisciplinary designer who can bridge the gap between creativity and technology, creating products that are both beautiful and useful while continuously learning throughout my career.
